# Grading and results

Marks and grade points use Decimal. Percentage and grade-point outputs round
half up to two decimal places. Subjects use raw totals or weights totaling 100.
Band boundaries are lower-inclusive and the greatest satisfied boundary wins.
Grade scheme versions have their own effective dates and cannot be edited after
use. Missing marks stay incomplete. Exempt and excused components leave the
denominator. An explicit absence counts as zero. Withheld components prevent
normal publication.

Official cards use publication snapshots. Withdrawal changes which publication
is current. A correction retains the previous mark, new mark, actor, and reason.
Recalculation and approval precede republication.

## Calculation

Raw subjects add obtained and available marks. Weighted subjects use
obtained divided by component maximum, multiplied by component weight. A
weighted subject contributes up to 100 weighted points to the overall total.
Excluded components remove their raw points or their weights. The overall
percentage divides total obtained points by total available points. GPA is the
mean of graded subject points when every included subject provides points.
The overall pass requires passing subjects and a passing overall grade.

Positions can be disabled, dense, or competition ranked. Position groups use
matching included subjects and denominators and can split by section.
Positions are shown for passing results only. The high-achiever report uses
an 80 percent threshold.

Draft results can be calculated before all marks are verified. Approval and
publication require verified submissions and a complete pass/fail outcome for
every rostered student. An authorized correction requires withdrawal of the
current class publication. The withdrawn result remains printable with an
explicit historical label. A corrected result becomes official after fresh
approval and publication.
